Weekly prep ~60 min
1
Lentils (20-25 min)
2 cups in water with bay leaf and salt. Drain and store. Makes 5 portions. Freeze half.
2
Chickpeas (45 min - remojar domingo)
Soak from Sunday. Cook 45 min. Drain. Freeze el viernes the leftovers.
3
Quinoa (15 min)
2 cups in 4 cups water. 15 min. Cool and store. Lasts 5 days.
4
Carrot grated
Rallar 3-4 carrots. Tupper con tapa. Lasts 5 days.
5
Eggs hard-boiled x4
Boil 10 min. Cool. Guardar con cascara hasta 5 days.
6
Pudding of chia (cada night antes of apertura)
3 tbsp chia + 200ml coconut milk + stevia. Mix. Fridge min 4h. Nights:: dom, lun 1/6, sab 6/6.
